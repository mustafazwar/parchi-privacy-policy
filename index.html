Privacy Policy

Parchi - Bluetooth Thermal Printer

Effective date: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Parchi ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application that allows users to preview and print documents and images using compatible printers. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Documents and Images

Parchi allows users to select or share documents and images, including PDF files, for previewing and printing. These files are processed locally on your device to provide the requested features. The standard printing functionality does not upload these files to our servers.

3. Bluetooth Permissions

Parchi may request Bluetooth permissions to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible printers. Bluetooth access is used to provide printing functionality. Printer communication takes place between your device and the selected printer.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Parchi does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. If we introduce advertising, analytics, or other third-party services in a future version, we will review and update this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures to reflect their data practices.

5. Data Collection and Sharing

The standard printing functionality does not require users to create an account or submit documents to our servers. We do not sell users' personal information. If future versions introduce features or third-party services that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

6. Data Storage and Security

Documents selected for printing are handled locally on your device as part of the standard printing process. Files you save or retain may remain on your device until you delete them. You are responsible for managing files stored on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Parchi is a general-purpose printing utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updated policies will be published on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
